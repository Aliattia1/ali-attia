Consoleya, Downtown Cairo1:100 / 1:2002025/26

A home for content creators inside Consoleya, the 1925 former French Consulate in Downtown Cairo. The historic shell stays. Inside, the floors are folded around a carved void and a giant display wall, so recording, editing, exhibiting and living all happen in one continuous vertical journey.

01 · Context

Downtown, full of memory

Downtown Cairo was laid out in the 1870s as the “Paris of the East”. It declined, changed hands and filled with cheap retail, and since the 2000s it has come back as a home for artists and thinkers. What stood out to me is that it feels full of memory but never silent: old walls, busy streets.

Consoleya sits on Al Fadl Street, a quiet pocket a few steps from everything. Today it is a coworking building in the Al Ismaelia portfolio. That made it a natural fit for a content creator hub, with vlog and street content right outside the door.

02 · Concept

The fold & the void

The void is not space left over. It turns production into something you can watch.

Folding lets the project move beyond a refurbishment. Slabs are cut so the lower four levels share one field of vision around the central void and the exhibition wall. Floors stop being isolated and become one connected creative space.

On the ground floor the façade windows were enlarged and rebalanced so the display wall can be seen from the street. The city doesn’t end at the façade; it walks in. The building becomes a spatial portrait of the neo-nomad content creator: always moving, with a solid centre.

04 · Interiors

Old shell, new layer

Aged plaster, brick and concrete carry the history. Glass, black metal, terrazzo and oak are the new layer. The stair is a place to look from, not only a way up: wooden treads, glass handrails, and the void always in view.

A-200 · Creative consolidation

Swiss Heights

A home away from home · Dormitory for Swiss students

Students’ lounge1:100 / 1:200Residential

A sliced slide becomes a walkable path. A bold red entry pulls students through lounges, a gallery, a café and an arcade. One half of the slide frames views with its circular form, the other invites movement, and its tip becomes seating. Fun, function and Swiss identity in one gesture.

Type
Student dormitory
Floors 2–5
Rooms around a circular void
Third floor
Cultural centre & gallery
Signature
The red slide

Spaces

Red as a route

The cultural centre on the third floor brings Swiss and international students together through exhibitions, workshops and events, mixing Swiss minimalism with interactive pieces and links to the lake and public areas.

The dormitory spans floors two to five, with four double rooms per floor. Every room looks onto the circular interior void. Some floors add outdoor seating, laundry, kitchens and indoor dining.

A-300 · Collage & vision

Anastasia

Collage & vision · after Italo Calvino

Anastasia is a city of jewels, gardens and water that awakens every desire at once. The trap: while you think you are enjoying it, you are a slave to what you want. These AI-aided collages test how space, structure and barriers invite people in and then steer them.
